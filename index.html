Welcome to Take Me Home!

Paragliding Transit Navigator • v1.6

Finds reachable public transport stops from your landing spot or glide horizon and calculates the fastest public transport route back home.

1
Set your Destination (Home)
Long-press on mobile (or right-click on desktop) anywhere on the map and choose "🏁 Set Destination".
2
Live GPS or Set Pilot Position
Tap to acquire your live 3D GPS position, or long-press on mobile (right-click on desktop) on the map and select "🪂 Set Pilot Position" to simulate any spot.
3
Inspect Stops & Timetables
Colored markers show stops ranked by arrival time. Tap any stop to see glide ratio (L/D), departure times, and direct links to SBB / Google Maps.
4
Install Shortcut on Mobile
Open ⚙️ Settings and tap "Install and create shortcut" for quick navigation whenever you land.
